5. Packs

5.1 What a pack is. Each pack is a purchase of the specific number of physical trading cards stated on the pack’s page (for example, one card, or five cards for a Booster). Each card is a real, authentic card drawn from the pack’s published card pool. When you buy a pack, Cardbit assigns your card or cards from the cards remaining in that pool and records the result to your account before you see the reveal.

5.2 Pools, odds and value. Each pack page lists the card pool, the rarity tiers and the current odds for each tier, which are calculated from the complete packs that remain unclaimed in that pool. Odds change as packs are opened and when a pool is restocked. Cardbit may add cards to a pool (including new stock and cards sold back to Cardbit) or remove cards from it, but only in a published restock: each restock creates a numbered pool version, shown on the pack page with every card it contains and a SHA-256 fingerprint, and each opening records the pool version it was drawn from. Each paid opening is drawn with a committed server seed, your client seed and an opening number, and you can verify the draw after revealing the seed on the Verified Shuffle page. Packs have no minimum card value. You can search the pool on each pack page to see every card it contains, including the lowest-valued card, before you buy. Tiers are assigned by each card's value rank within its current pool; tier cutoffs are updated at each published restock and once a day as prices change, and a pulled card keeps the tier it had when pulled. Any reference values, rarity names or tier labels are for information only and are not a promise of any value or resale price. Cards in a pool may vary by language, set, printing, finish and condition as listed.

5.3 The reveal. The reveal animation shows a result that was already assigned and stored. Skipping, replaying or refreshing the reveal, or closing the app, does not change your result or give you another card. If the reveal fails to load, your card is still in My Vault.

5.5 Availability. Packs are subject to availability. If a pool runs out or a pack cannot be fulfilled after you pay, Cardbit will return the Cardbits you spent on that pack to your balance.

5.6 No guarantee of outcome. You are buying the card or cards stated on the pack page, not a chance to win anything. Disappointment with the specific card assigned is not a reason for a refund.

This Refund Policy is part of the Cardbit Terms of Service. Cardbit sells authentic physical trading cards. Because each pack is assigned and recorded the moment you buy it, and Cardbits can be spent instantly, most purchases are final. This policy explains exactly when Cardbit will make it right.

1. At a Glance

  • Cardbits purchases: final sale. Cardbits are not refundable or redeemable for cash.
  • Packs and singles: final once purchased. Not liking a card is not a reason for a refund.
  • Buybacks: final once accepted.
  • Shipping charges: refundable only if you cancel before the shipment is processed, or if Cardbit cannot ship.
  • Cardbit’s mistakes: we always fix duplicate charges, incorrect charges, unfulfilled orders and wrong cards shipped.

2. Cardbits

All purchases of Cardbits, including packages with bonus Cardbits, are final and non-refundable, whether or not the Cardbits have been spent. Cardbits cannot be exchanged for cash, cashed out, or transferred to another account. The only exceptions are:

  • Duplicate or incorrect charge. If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, Cardbit will refund the extra amount to your original payment method.
  • Cardbits not credited. If your payment succeeded but the Cardbits never appeared, Cardbit will credit them or refund the payment.
  • Unauthorized purchase. If someone used your payment method without permission, contact us right away. After review, unspent Cardbits from that purchase will be removed and the payment refunded.
  • Where required by law. Nothing in this policy limits a refund that applicable law requires.

3. Packs

Each pack is the purchase of the specific physical card or cards assigned to you from the published pool, and the result is recorded before the reveal. Pack purchases are final and cannot be cancelled, exchanged or refunded because of the card you receive, its value, its rarity, a change of mind, or skipping or replaying the reveal. You can review the full pool and current odds before buying. If a pack cannot be fulfilled after you pay (for example, the pool sold out at the same moment), Cardbit will return the Cardbits you spent to your balance.

4. Singles

Singles purchases are final. If the card in your vault does not match the listing you bought (wrong card, printing, language or condition tier), contact us within 14 days and Cardbit will replace it with the correct card or return the Cardbits you spent.

5. Buybacks

When you accept a buyback offer, the card is sold to Cardbit and the Cardbits are added to your balance. Accepted buybacks are final and cannot be reversed, even if the offer later changes.

6. Shipments

  • Cancel before processing: you can cancel a shipment request until it is processed. The shipping charge is returned in the form you paid it (Cardbits back to your balance, or a refund to your payment method), and the cards return to your vault.
  • Lost in transit: if the carrier shows a package as lost, or it has no delivery scan and the carrier confirms it cannot be found, Cardbit will replace the cards with the same cards in the same or better condition. If an identical card is not available, Cardbit will credit Cardbits equal to its buyback offer at the time, increased by 25%. Lost packages are replaced, not refunded in cash.
  • Marked delivered: if tracking shows the package delivered to the address you provided, Cardbit’s responsibility ends. For a package that was stolen, damaged or tampered with after delivery, you must file a claim with the carrier (and, if you wish, a police report). Cardbit will share tracking and shipment records to help.
  • Wrong card shipped: if Cardbit ships a card that is not the card in your vault request, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. Cardbit will send a prepaid label and ship the correct card at no cost.
  • Wrong address: Cardbit is not responsible for packages sent to an incorrect address you entered. A returned package will go back to your vault, and a new shipping charge applies to reship it.

7. Returns

Because cards are shipped from cards you already own in My Vault, Cardbit does not accept returns of shipped cards, except a wrong card shipped by Cardbit under Section 6. You can sell eligible cards back to Cardbit for Cardbits before shipping them.

8. Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can usually fix a problem the same day. If a payment is disputed, charged back or reversed after Cardbits from it have been credited, Cardbit will:

  1. remove the related Cardbits (including bonus Cardbits from that package) from your balance;
  2. if your balance is not enough, return cards in your vault obtained with those Cardbits, or other vaulted cards of equal or greater buyback value, to Cardbit’s inventory to cover the difference;
  3. lock your account while the dispute is open; and
  4. treat any remaining shortfall as an amount you owe Cardbit.

Cardbit may share account, purchase, reveal, vault and shipment records with the payment processor and your card issuer to respond to a dispute. Accounts with false or repeated disputes may be closed under the Terms of Service.

9. Time Limits

Report a billing error or unauthorized charge within 60 days of the charge, a lost package within 30 days of the expected delivery date, and a wrong card within 7 days of delivery. Requests after these windows may not be reviewed, except where the law requires otherwise.

10. How to Request Help

Email Support@cardbit.com from the email on your account with your order or shipment number, the card(s) affected, a short description and any photos. Keep the packaging for shipping issues. Please do not send full card numbers or ID documents by email. We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds go to the original payment method and may take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

Shipping PolicyCosts, Timing, Lost Packages

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Shipping Policy is part of the Cardbit Terms of Service. Opening a pack or buying a single puts the physical card in your secure Cardbit vault. Nothing ships until you ask. You choose which cards to ship and when.

1. Where We Ship

Cardbit ships only to addresses in the United States. We do not ship to addresses in Alabama, Hawaii, Idaho, Minnesota, Nevada, New York, Utah or Washington, or outside the United States. We may decline any address that we cannot verify or that a carrier cannot serve.

2. Shipping Cost

  • $6.00 flat, or 600 Cardbits, per shipment, for any number of cards up to 30.
  • Free shipping for any single shipment of 30 or more cards, or of cards worth 10,000 Cardbits or more in total.
  • Cardbit does not currently charge sales tax on shipping. If that changes for your state, it will be shown before you confirm.

Combine cards from as many packs and purchases as you like into one shipment to save.

3. Processing Time

Shipments are processed within 48 hours of your request. Processing means your cards are pulled from the vault, checked against your request, protectively packed and handed to the carrier. Requests made during holidays, severe weather, carrier disruptions or unusually high volume may take longer; we will email you if a shipment is delayed. To protect collectors from fraud, an account may get a short security review before its cards ship, usually within one business day; while it does, your cards stay safe in your vault.

4. Carriers, Tracking and Delivery

Cardbit chooses the carrier and service, typically USPS, UPS or FedEx. Every shipment is tracked. You will receive an email with a tracking number when the carrier issues it, and tracking appears in My Vault under Shipments. Delivery times are estimates set by the carrier and are not guaranteed. Cardbit may require a signature for high-value shipments.

5. Your Address

Please check your shipping address before you confirm. You can change it until the shipment is processed. Cardbit is not responsible for packages shipped to an incorrect or incomplete address you entered. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable, the cards go back into your vault and a new shipping charge applies to reship.

6. Cancelling a Shipment

You can cancel a shipment request at any time before it is processed. The cards return to your vault and the shipping charge is returned the way you paid it. Once processed, a shipment cannot be cancelled.

7. Locked Cards

Cards in a pending shipment are locked. They cannot be sold back to Cardbit or added to another shipment until the shipment is cancelled.

8. Lost Packages

Every shipment is insured while in transit. If the carrier shows your package as lost, or the package has no delivery scan and the carrier confirms it cannot be found, Cardbit will replace the cards with the same cards in the same or better condition. If an identical card is not available, Cardbit will credit Cardbits equal to its buyback offer at the time, increased by 25%. Please contact us if tracking has not updated for 10 days. Lost-package claims must be made within 30 days of the expected delivery date. Lost packages are replaced, not refunded in cash.

9. Delivered, Stolen or Damaged Packages

Risk of loss passes to you when the carrier marks the package delivered to the address you provided. If tracking shows delivered and the package is missing, stolen, damaged or tampered with, you must file a claim directly with the carrier, and you may file a police report. Cardbit will provide the tracking number, shipment contents and any other records you need. Please photograph the package and contents and keep all packaging.

10. Wrong Card Received

If the cards you receive are not the cards you requested from your vault, email us within 7 days of delivery with photos. Cardbit will send a prepaid return label and ship the correct cards at no cost.

11. Inactive Vaults

If your account has no activity for 12 consecutive months, Cardbit will email you. If you do not respond within 30 days, Cardbit may ship your vaulted cards to your most recent shipping address, with the standard shipping charge taken from your Cardbits balance where available, or buy them back at the current offer, as described in the Terms of Service.

12. Contact

Questions about a shipment? Email Support@cardbit.com with your shipment number. Cardbit LLC · 181 Hughes Road, Suite 5, Madison, AL 35758.
