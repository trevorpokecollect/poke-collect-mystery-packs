The drawing for each Entry Period happens automatically at or shortly after 8:00:00 PM CT when that Entry Period closes. Entries do not carry over from one Entry Period to the next, except as stated for the one-time Whatnot bonus in B2.

B5. Drawing and Odds

The winner is selected at random from all eligible entries for that Entry Period, weighted by each entrant's number of entries, using a verifiable process: before each Entry Period opens, Sponsor publishes a cryptographic commitment of a random seed; after it closes, the seed and a snapshot of pseudonymous entry totals reproduce the result. Accounts on hold under Section A10 are not eligible. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received for that Entry Period. If no eligible entries are received, no prize is awarded for that Entry Period.

C1. How It Works

Every eligible account gets one free spin per day on each spinner tier it has unlocked (Section C3). Each day runs from 12:00:00 AM CT to 11:59:59 PM CT. No purchase is needed to spin. The result is decided by Sponsor's server at the moment of the spin and stored. Spinning again, refreshing or replaying the same day shows the stored result and never awards another prize.

C2. How to Spin

Online: sign in, open the Rewards page, confirm you are 18 or older, and tap Spin on each unlocked tier. By Mail: send a postcard marked "Daily Spinner" under Section A5. On the day Sponsor logs your postcard, Sponsor runs one spin on each tier you have unlocked and have not yet spun that day, using the same process as an online spin, and the results appear in your account. If you have already used every unlocked tier that day, Sponsor runs them on the next day you have not spun. Limit: one (1) spin per tier per person per day, whether online or by mail, and one (1) Mail-In postcard per person per day.
